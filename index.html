I'm Anas — White Hunter, a cybersecurity learner focused on offensive security and the process behind a successful attack: enumerate, understand, exploit, escalate, pivot, and document.

I learn best by getting my hands dirty. Most of my progress comes from Hack The Box, TryHackMe, pwn.college, CTFs and personal labs where I can turn a concept into an actual attack path instead of memorising a tool or a command.
